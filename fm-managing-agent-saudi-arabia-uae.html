FM Managing Agent

Crossfield Engineering Services acts as the asset owner’s FM managing agent (FMMA) in Saudi Arabia and the UAE, overseeing facilities management contracts and service-provider performance.

Send an RFP or invitation

What Crossfield can deliver

  • Service-provider oversight

    Inspect sites, review planned and reactive maintenance records, and track corrective actions across hard and soft FM providers.

  • Performance and payment review

    Validate KPI and SLA evidence, verify invoices and variations, and review deductions in line with the contract.

  • Portfolio reporting and continuity

    Report cost, performance and operational risk by site. Track mobilisation, handover issues and recurring failures through to closure.

Tenders and prequalification

For a current or upcoming FM managing agent appointment, share your portfolio size, service structure and target start date. Procurement teams can send expressions of interest, prequalification questionnaires and RFP invitations to bd@crossfield-engienering.com.
