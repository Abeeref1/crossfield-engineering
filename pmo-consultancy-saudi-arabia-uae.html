PMO Consultancy

Crossfield Engineering Services sets up and supports project management offices (PMOs) for owners and developers managing portfolios in Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

Send an RFP or invitation

What Crossfield can deliver

  • PMO design and mobilisation

    Define governance, decision rights, stage gates and reporting requirements across the project portfolio.

  • Programme and project controls

    Set consistent standards for baselines, schedule and cost reporting, cash-flow forecasts, procurement milestones, risks and changes.

  • Portfolio assurance

    Review programme health, identify decisions holding up delivery and give leadership a consolidated view of priorities, exposure and recovery actions.

Tenders and prequalification

For a PMO appointment or project-controls package, share your portfolio size, delivery stages and reporting priorities. Procurement teams can send expressions of interest, prequalification questionnaires and RFP invitations to bd@crossfield-engienering.com.
