Riyadh Giga-Project PMO & Programme Controls

Crossfield supports complex Saudi capital programmes with PMO/PMC, programme controls, design management, risk registers, cost and commercial integration and executive reporting.

Programme controls

Crossfield’s programme-controls focus includes schedule governance, baseline assurance, progress, forecasting, risk, change, cost visibility and management reporting across large multi-package environments.

Design and delivery management

Complex programmes require design authority, interface control, decision tracking, procurement alignment and clear ownership across consultants, contractors and client teams.

Executive reporting

The objective is to compress complex programme information into reliable management decisions: what is late, why, what is financially exposed, what decision is needed and who owns the action.
