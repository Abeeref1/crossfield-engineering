Where Crossfield fits

Crossfield works across the space between strategy and execution: programme governance, PMO and PMC structures, planning and controls, cost and commercial visibility, delivery assurance, management reporting, procurement interfaces and executive decision support.

The emphasis is not on adding another reporting layer. It is on creating reliable management information, clear authority, accountable actions and earlier intervention when schedule, cost, change or delivery performance moves off plan.

Typical mandates

Relevant mandates include PMO mobilisation, PMC support, schedule and controls assurance, independent project health checks, recovery planning, executive dashboards, commercial integration, project governance, cost-control reviews and portfolio reporting.
