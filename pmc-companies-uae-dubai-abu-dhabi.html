When a specialist may outperform a larger PMC

A specialist can be the better fit when speed, senior attention and independence matter more than staffing scale. Typical examples include a delayed package, weak baseline governance, fragmented executive reporting, procurement slippage, claims exposure, transition to operations, FM mobilisation or digital transformation of project controls. The scope is then defined around measurable management outcomes rather than headcount.
