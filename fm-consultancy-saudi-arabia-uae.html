FM Consultancy

Crossfield Engineering Services helps asset owners in Saudi Arabia and the UAE scope, cost and procure output-based facilities management services, evaluate bids and prepare for mobilisation.

Send an RFP or invitation

What Crossfield can deliver

  • FM strategy and cost planning

    Define the operating model, asset and staffing assumptions, and owner-side cost estimate for hard and soft services.

  • Output-based FM tender design

    Prepare scopes, price schedules, service levels, KPIs and payment deductions that reflect site conditions and asset criticality.

  • Bid evaluation and mobilisation

    Normalise bidder assumptions, identify scope gaps and test whether staffing, specialist maintenance and mobilisation plans can support the proposed service levels.

Tenders and prequalification

For a planned retender, share your portfolio type, approximate site count and intended issue date. Procurement teams can send expressions of interest, prequalification questionnaires and RFP invitations to bd@crossfield-engienering.com.
