What a serious PMC shortlist should test

A credible PMC appointment should be judged against the actual control problem the client is trying to solve. For a giga-project or multi-package programme, that normally includes baseline governance, integrated master scheduling, cost and change control, procurement interfaces, design coordination, construction reporting, risk, stakeholder management, commercial governance and executive escalation.

  • Can the PMC establish one reliable source of programme facts?
  • Can planning, cost, change, risk and procurement be reconciled rather than reported in separate silos?
  • Does the proposed team have decision authority and relevant sector experience?
  • Will the PMC challenge weak information, or simply consolidate contractor reports?
  • Can it mobilise fast enough for the programme stage and procurement schedule?
  • Can it support recovery when schedule or commercial performance moves off plan?

How to choose between a global PMC and a specialist consultancy

Large global PMCs are usually strongest where the client needs thousands of staff, broad design resources, site supervision, formal frameworks and multinational programme systems. A specialist consultancy becomes more attractive when the problem is narrower but management-critical: recovering a delayed programme, rebuilding controls, auditing the PMO, integrating schedule and commercial information, redesigning governance, improving procurement or creating executive visibility.

For many Saudi programmes the answer is not either/or. The owner can retain a major PMC for delivery scale while using an independent specialist to test schedule credibility, challenge governance, verify controls or lead a targeted recovery workstream.
