Crossfield Engineering · Independent Assurance

Project Assurance, Recovery & Delay Risk

Crossfield provides independent challenge when a project is late, commercially exposed, poorly controlled or difficult to understand from existing reports.

Independent project health checks

Crossfield can review programme authority, critical path, progress integrity, forecast completion, cost and change exposure, procurement, interfaces, risks and management actions to establish the real project position.

Recovery planning

Recovery requires more than a compressed schedule. Crossfield examines logic, resources, workfronts, procurement, design, approvals, interfaces and commercial constraints to identify credible interventions.

Delay and schedule risk

Relevant work includes baseline and update review, critical-path analysis, delay-event mapping, windows analysis support, entitlement evidence, near-critical monitoring and executive schedule assurance.
