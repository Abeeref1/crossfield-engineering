Project Management Consultancy (PMC)

Crossfield Engineering Services supports owners and developers with project management consultancy (PMC) in Saudi Arabia and the UAE, from design coordination and procurement through construction and operational handover.

Send an RFP or invitation

What Crossfield can deliver

  • Design and procurement coordination

    Track design deliverables, approvals, tender packages and procurement dependencies against the programme.

  • Construction delivery and reporting

    Monitor consultant and contractor performance, review progress and recovery plans, and escalate schedule, cost and interface risks to the owner.

  • Handover and operational readiness

    Plan the transition to operations by tracking commissioning, warranties, O&M documentation, asset data and FM mobilisation.

Tenders and prequalification

For an upcoming project or recovery assignment, share the project location, stage and the owner support required. Procurement teams can send expressions of interest, prequalification questionnaires and RFP invitations to bd@crossfield-engienering.com.
