Saudi Arabia and UAE FM requirements

Major developments across Saudi Arabia and the UAE increasingly require FM thinking during design and construction, not after handover. Crossfield helps connect maintainability, asset data, warranties, O&M documentation, lifecycle assumptions, commissioning and operational readiness so the operating organisation is not left to reconstruct the asset picture after completion.
