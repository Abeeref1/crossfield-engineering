Crossfield Engineering · Saudi Arabia · UAE · GCC

Engineering Consultancy, PMC, Construction Management, FM, AI & Digital Transformation

Crossfield Engineering is a senior-led engineering and project-delivery advisory business supporting owners, developers, operators and contractors across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the GCC. Core capability covers project management consultancy (PMC), PMO, programme and project controls, construction management, commercial and contracts, procurement and tendering, facilities and asset management, project recovery, data centres, AI-enabled operations and digital transformation.

For major programmes, Crossfield focuses on the management problems that decide outcomes: whether the baseline is credible, whether procurement supports the programme, whether cost and change reconcile to schedule, whether risks have accountable actions, whether executive reporting identifies the real decision, and whether handover connects construction with operational readiness. The model can support an owner directly, complement a large PMC or provide independent challenge on a focused recovery or assurance mandate.
