Facilities Management for Industrial & Logistics Assets

Crossfield supports industrial, logistics and complex operating environments with hard services, soft services, FM consultancy, lifecycle planning and asset-performance strategy.

Hard and soft FM

Crossfield supports operating-model design, hard-services strategy, soft-services delivery, service specifications, contractor models, mobilisation, KPIs and performance governance.

Lifecycle and asset planning

Lifecycle planning connects condition, criticality, maintenance strategy, replacement forecasting, capital planning and operating risk so asset decisions are financially and operationally defensible.

Industrial and logistics environments

Industrial and logistics assets often require stronger control of critical equipment, uptime, access, statutory compliance, maintenance windows and operating continuity. Crossfield structures these requirements into the FM strategy.
