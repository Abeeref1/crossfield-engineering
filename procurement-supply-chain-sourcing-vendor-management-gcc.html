Procurement, Supply Chain & Strategic Sourcing

Crossfield helps organisations structure procurement around technical compliance, commercial value, supply risk, lead time and delivery outcomes rather than price alone.

Strategic sourcing

Crossfield supports category strategy, market analysis, sourcing plans, technical-commercial evaluation, supplier shortlisting, competitive tendering and negotiation preparation.

Supplier and subcontractor governance

The focus includes prequalification, technical compliance, country-of-origin requirements, capacity, lead time, quality, commercial risk, performance management and supplier concentration.

Project procurement

For capital programmes, procurement must align with design maturity, schedule logic, long-lead items, interfaces, approval gates and project cash flow. Crossfield connects procurement strategy to the programme rather than treating it as a standalone purchasing exercise.
