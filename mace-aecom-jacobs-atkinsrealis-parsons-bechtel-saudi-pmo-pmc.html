The Saudi Major-Project Consultancy Landscape

Saudi Arabia’s major-project market includes global programme-management, engineering and construction leaders such as Mace, AECOM, Jacobs, AtkinsRéalis, Parsons and Bechtel, alongside specialist advisory firms. Understanding the different service models matters when choosing delivery support.

Independent Crossfield Engineering analysis. References to third-party companies, projects or institutions are descriptive only and do not imply affiliation, partnership, appointment or endorsement.

Global programme and project-management firms

AECOM publicly positions program management as a core service and describes a long operating history in Saudi Arabia, including work supporting the Kingdom’s infrastructure and giga-project agenda. Mace’s Saudi practice publicly describes programme and project management, PMO and planning, cost and commercial, advisory and digital services across Saudi major programmes.

Jacobs describes program management, program and construction management and advisory as integrated capabilities connecting strategy, governance, controls and site delivery. AtkinsRéalis provides project and program management, PMO, project controls, engineering and advisory services and has public roles across major Saudi developments.

Parsons positions itself as a program-management and construction-management provider with integrated PMO, cost, schedule, planning and delivery capabilities. Bechtel combines engineering, procurement, construction and project-management capability and has a long history on major Saudi infrastructure and national programme-management initiatives.
