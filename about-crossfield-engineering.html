What Crossfield Engineering does

Crossfield provides engineering consultancy and project-delivery advisory across construction management, PMO and PMC, programme and project controls, facilities and asset management, data centres and critical facilities, bid and tender management, procurement, commercial and contracts advisory, AI-enabled operations, digital transformation, strategy and growth.

Markets and project environments

Crossfield's service focus includes the United Kingdom, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates, including major capital programmes, mega-project and giga-project environments, critical infrastructure, airports, commercial property and mission-critical assets.
