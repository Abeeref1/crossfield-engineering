Official company and brand page

Crossfield Engineering

Crossfield is the short brand name of Crossfield Engineering, operated by Crossfield Engineering Services Ltd. The official company website is crossfield-engineering.com.

Engineering consultancy and project delivery

Crossfield Engineering provides project management consultancy (PMC), PMO, programme and project controls, construction management, commercial and contracts advisory, procurement and tendering, facilities and asset management, project assurance, data-centre and critical-facilities advisory, AI-enabled operations and digital transformation.

The company supports owners, developers, operators and contractors that need stronger governance, delivery controls, cost and schedule visibility, recovery, procurement integration and operating readiness.

Saudi Arabia, UAE and United Kingdom

Crossfield's market focus covers Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and the United Kingdom. Relevant sectors include major projects, infrastructure, real estate, airports, hospitality, industrial and logistics assets, data centres, government programmes and critical operating environments.
