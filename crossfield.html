Official brand page

Crossfield

Crossfield is the short brand name of Crossfield Engineering, the engineering consultancy and project-delivery advisory business operating at crossfield-engineering.com.

What Crossfield does

Crossfield Engineering provides engineering consultancy, PMO and PMC, project controls, cost and commercial management, construction management, facilities and asset management, fitout and refurbishment, tendering and RFP advisory, procurement, data-centre and critical-facilities advisory, AI-enabled operations, BIM and digital transformation, strategy, growth and restructuring.

Where Crossfield works

The company’s market focus includes the United Kingdom, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates, with relevance to infrastructure, real estate, giga and mega projects, airports, data centres, hospitality, industrial and logistics assets, government and public-sector programmes.
